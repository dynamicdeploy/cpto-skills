# Reference: Product Launch Plan

## Purpose
Generate a company-specific, complete product launch checklist Excel based on the Ultimate Product
Launch Checklist Template, with all phases, owners, and dates pre-populated for the specific product.

## Source Template
Based on `Ultimate_Product_Launch_Checklist_Template.xlsx → 'CHECKLIST'` tab.

---

## Launch Phase Structure

### Phase 1: Early Planning Stage

**Strategic Planning**
- Define Goals of Launch (business metrics, success criteria)
- Produce PRD (Product Requirements Document)
- Produce MRD (Market Requirements Document)
- Conduct Market Research & Competitive Analysis
- Develop Product Positioning & Messaging Framework
- Develop Go-to-Market Plan (Launch Strategy)
- Set Tentative Launch Date & Preliminary Timeline
- Share Upcoming Launch with Organization (internal teaser)

**Customer & Market Validation**
- Identify Early Access / Beta customers
- Conduct customer discovery interviews (min. 10)
- Define Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Define buyer personas and decision-maker map
- Validate pricing and packaging assumptions
- Establish Customer Advisory Board (CAB) participation

**Legal & Compliance Preparation**
- IP review (patents, trademarks)
- Legal review of contracts/ToS for new features
- Data privacy impact assessment (DPIA)
- Compliance certification readiness check

---

### Phase 2: Pre-Launch

**Launch Promotion Efforts**
- Develop launch messaging and narrative
- Create launch landing page / product page
- Write launch blog post (draft)
- Develop customer-facing release notes
- Produce demo video / product walkthrough
- Develop pitch deck / sales deck updates
- Create FAQ document (internal and external)
- Social media content calendar
- PR/Analyst outreach plan

**Engineering & Product Readiness**
- Feature freeze date confirmed
- All P0/P1 bugs resolved
- Performance testing completed (load/stress)
- Security review completed and sign-off
- Accessibility review (WCAG 2.1 AA)
- Internationalization/localization complete
- Documentation (API docs, user guides) complete
- Feature flags configured for launch rollout
- Rollback plan documented and tested
- Staging environment mirrors production

**Sales Enablement**
- Sales training completed
- Battle cards updated for competitive handling
- Demo environment configured
- Pricing approval and documentation
- Proposal/quote templates updated
- CRM updated with new product data

**Marketing Readiness**
- Email campaigns scheduled
- Paid campaign creative approved and scheduled
- Partner co-marketing materials ready
- Event/conference presence planned (if applicable)
- Analyst briefings scheduled (Gartner, Forrester, etc.)
- Press release drafted and approved

**Customer Success Readiness**
- CS team trained on new capabilities
- Support documentation updated
- Escalation path for launch-day issues defined
- Customer communication plan (existing users)
- Beta customer references confirmed

---

### Phase 3: Launch Day

**Launch Day Execution**
- Go/No-Go decision meeting held
- Launch command center staffed
- Engineering on-call confirmed
- Marketing content published (blog, social, email)
- Press release distributed
- Internal announcement sent (all-hands, Slack)
- Partner notifications sent
- Customer announcement sent
- Website updated with new product information
- Monitoring dashboards live and reviewed

**Day-of Metrics Tracking**
- Website traffic / conversion monitored
- Sign-up / trial activation rate tracked
- Error rate and latency monitored
- Customer support ticket volume tracked
- Social media engagement tracked
- Pipeline generated by launch tracked

---

### Phase 4: Post-Launch

**Immediate Post-Launch (Week 1)**
- Launch retrospective meeting held
- Bug triage from launch-day issues
- Customer feedback collected (survey, support tickets)
- Usage metrics reviewed
- Press/analyst coverage tracked and amplified
- Social media engagement continued

**30-60-90 Day Review**
- Product adoption metrics reviewed
- NPS/CSAT survey sent to new users
- Sales pipeline from launch analyzed
- Competitive response assessed
- Roadmap updated based on launch learnings
- Case study development started (reference customers)
- Second-wave content published

## Excel Schema

### Tab: "Launch Plan"
| Column | Header | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| A | # | Sequential number |
| B | Task | Task description |
| C | Phase | Early Planning / Pre-Launch / Launch Day / Post-Launch |
| D | Category | Strategic / Engineering / Marketing / Sales / CS / Legal |
| E | Responsible Party | Owner name or role |
| F | Status | Not Started / In Progress / On Hold / Done / Blocked |
| G | Target Date | Planned completion |
| H | Actual Date | Actual completion |
| I | Priority | P0 / P1 / P2 |
| J | Notes | Context, links, decisions |

### Tab: "Launch Metrics"
| Column | Header | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| A | Metric | KPI name |
| B | Category | Awareness / Acquisition / Activation / Revenue |
| C | Baseline | Pre-launch value |
| D | Target | Launch target |
| E | Actual | Actual result |
| F | Status | On Track / At Risk / Off Track |
| G | Owner | Metric owner |

**Standard Launch Metrics to Include:**
- Website traffic (Awareness)
- Trial signups (Acquisition)
- Trial-to-paid conversion % (Acquisition)
- Time-to-first-value (Activation)
- Feature adoption rate (Activation)
- Pipeline generated ($) (Revenue)
- New logos from launch (Revenue)
- NPS of new users (Satisfaction)
- Support ticket volume (Quality)
- P0/P1 bugs post-launch (Quality)
- Media/analyst mentions (Awareness)

## User Input Collection

Ask the user:
1. **Product Name & Description**: "What is being launched?"
2. **Launch Date**: "What is the target launch date?"
3. **Launch Scope**: "Is this a GA, beta, or limited release?"
4. **Key Teams**: "Who are the owners for Engineering, Product, Marketing, Sales, CS?"
5. **Compliance Requirements**: "Any specific compliance gates? (SOC2, HIPAA, GDPR)"
6. **Launch Scale**: "How many customers/users is this initial launch targeting?"
7. **Success Metrics**: "What are the 3-5 KPIs that define launch success?"

Use answers to:
- Pre-populate owner columns
- Set dates relative to launch date (work backwards)
- Include or exclude sections based on scope
- Add company-specific tasks in relevant sections

---

## Date Calculation Pattern

Work backwards from launch date:
- Launch Day = T+0
- Go/No-Go Meeting = T-3 business days
- Feature Freeze = T-10 business days
- Sales Enablement Complete = T-14 business days
- Marketing Materials Approved = T-7 business days
- Security Review Complete = T-14 business days
- Documentation Complete = T-7 business days
- Beta Customer Confirmation = T-21 business days

Use Excel formula: `=LaunchDate - N` where LaunchDate is a named cell.
